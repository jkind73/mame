SCSP FM voice mixing, native run against the pre-fix qualified binary
(803be0ad, sha256 from invocation.json), saturnjp, -nodrc, -sound none.

Method: two carriers play the same looped square wave from the same phase,
slot 0 with the FM registers programmed and panned left, slot 2 unmodulated
and panned right.  The distance between the two channels' rising edges is
the address shift in words.  A duty cycle comparison cannot see a constant
address offset: a wave form that repeats once per loop keeps its high and
low times, only its phase moves (this run replaced that method).

Defects this documents:
 * level >= 0x4000 (at or above half scale) flips the sign of the shift:
   the 16 bit sound stack write stored twice the slot output and wrapped
   (wrap5..wrap9b measure -16..-128 words where Table 4.17 gives +16..+384).
 * MDL 0-4 modulated anyway (Table 4.15 rate 0): wrap3 -4, wrap4 -8 words.
 * a SOUS word written by software is read as half depth (injA 252 for 504,
   inj9 126 for 252, injC 256 for 512): the read of the 16 bit file has to
   be the slot output, not the doubled internal value.
 * injF (0x3F00 at MDL=FH) drives the carrier out of its wave form data
   entirely, so no edges were measurable at all.

Cases m5..mA2, deepB, clipB and clipF pass in this run: below half scale
the old scaling already matched the table, which is why the fixture also
covers the wrap, the MDL 0-4 rate and the injected stack words.
